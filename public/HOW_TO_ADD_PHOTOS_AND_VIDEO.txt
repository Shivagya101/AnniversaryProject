HOW TO ADD YOUR PHOTOS & 30-SECOND VIDEO
========================================

1. 37 MONTHLY COLLAGE PHOTOS (Nov 2023 to Nov 2026):
   Place your 37 photos inside:
   public/photos/months/
   Named:
   - month-01.jpg  (November 2023)
   - month-02.jpg  (December 2023)
   ...
   - month-37.jpg  (November 2026)

   (Or edit `src/data/anniversaryData.js` to point to any custom filenames or URLs!)

2. REVEAL QUESTION PHOTOS:
   Place photos for the "Why do you think I love you?" reveal cards inside:
   public/photos/questions/
   Named:
   - reveal-1.jpg
   - reveal-2.jpg

3. YOUR 30-SECOND FINALE VIDEO:
   - Option A (Local file): Place your 30-sec video at `public/videos/anniversary-video.mp4`
   - Option B (External API): Open `vite.config.js` (line ~128) or `src/data/anniversaryData.js` (`FINALE_CONFIG.videoApiEndpoint`) and put your custom API URL!

4. WHERE HER ANSWERS ARE SAVED:
   Whenever she selects an MCQ option (like your anniversary outfit) or clicks "Yes, Forever",
   her choices are automatically saved into:
   - responses.txt  (human-readable summary file in the project root)
   - responses.json (structured JSON file in the project root)
